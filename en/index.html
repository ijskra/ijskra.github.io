About소개

Jung-Woong Oh is a composer and harpsichordist. In 2024 he premiered two orchestral works, Mingaramsori and Deluge. Following his Harpsichord Concerto No. 1, he composed and performed Harpsichord Concerto No. 2 and Suite for Harpsichord at Imagining the Harpsichord, his 2025 recital of new works.

At Architecture of Infinite Microtones, a concert supported by the Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture, he composed and performed 22 Years for retuned grand piano and two electronic playback devices, having earlier co-composed and performed Plaid Panorama. He has presented electronic works at IRCAM, the MA/IN Festival and ICMC 2018, and performed as pianist with electronics at ICMC 2024 Seoul.

As a harpsichordist he played basso continuo at the Nakdong Art Center Concert Hall pre-opening concert LE: CLASSIC Gibboni and at the Seocho Chamber Music Festival 2025. He was music director of the film From You (2023), which won the Grand Prize in the Korean Competition at the Jeonju International Film Festival, and composed and performed the music for the play A Drop of Me (2024), supported by the Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture. In 2026 he was music director of the film Not For You, invited to the 31st Busan International Film Festival. He currently teaches at Seoul National University, Chonnam National University and the Korea National University of Arts.
